The DigiPlus AI interface kit

Every element the product needs, in one place, so screens get assembled instead of invented. The point is not decoration: it is to remove the specific details that make a site read as machine-generated, and replace them with choices a person made on purpose.

Why the site reads as “AI made this”

Six tells, in the order people notice them. The first one is on your landing page right now.

What gives it away
  • Emoji as icons 🧠 💬 🎯 — the single loudest tell. Different artists, different grids, renders differently on every OS.
  • Inter for everything. The default font of generated sites. Correct, characterless.
  • Purple-to-blue gradient blobs floating behind a centred hero.
  • Everything centred, every card identical, perfectly even rhythm.
  • Invented proof — “Trusted by 10,000+” with no names.
  • Flat, clean, empty surfaces with no texture, no shadow logic, no depth.
What this kit does instead
  • One hand-drawn icon family — 24px grid, 1.7 stroke, round caps. 25 below, all editable SVG.
  • A display face for headlines, Inter kept for reading. Three candidates below.
  • Orange used as light, not as a blob: one warm source, tight glows, tinted surfaces.
  • Asymmetry on purpose — left-aligned headings, one wide card per row.
  • Real proof only. Your own screenshots, named clients, actual numbers.
  • Grain + layered surfaces + inner highlights so the dark feels like material.

This page has a film-grain layer over it. Compare it with the landing page — same colours, different feel.
