Going live with Meta

Until these are done, only people with a role on the Meta app get bot replies, and only their own Pages / numbers can connect. Manual connections with a business's own Meta app work already.

  1. Business verification — Business Settings → Security Center (needs your company or auto-entrepreneur documents).
  2. Privacy policy & terms URLs in App settings → Basic: and .
  3. Facebook Login for Business configurations: one for Pages/Instagram (permissions pages_show_list, pages_messaging, pages_manage_metadata, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_messages, business_management) and one for WhatsApp Embedded Signup (whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_messaging). Put their IDs in META_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID and META_WHATSAPP_CONFIG_ID.
  4. App Review for Advanced Access to the permissions above, with a screencast of connecting a Page and the bot answering.
  5. WhatsApp Tech Provider onboarding to use Embedded Signup for other businesses.
  6. Switch the app to Live mode.
