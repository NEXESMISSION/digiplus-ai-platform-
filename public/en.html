A question?
01What is an “AI reply”?

Every answer the assistant sends to a client counts as one reply. A normal sales conversation uses 5 to 10 replies. Summaries don't count.

02Can it invent prices or promises?

No. It only uses the prices, services and rules you write. When it doesn't know, it says the team will follow up and shares your contact details.

03Which languages does it speak?

It replies in the same language and alphabet the client writes in: Tunisian Derja in Arabic letters or in Arabizi (9adeh, 3andi), French, Arabic or English.

04What happens if I reach my monthly limit?

Your assistant stops answering and shows your contact details to clients. Add a reply pack or move to a bigger plan and it continues right away.

05Can I have several businesses?

Yes. Each assistant is one business, with its own knowledge, link and conversations. Pro includes 3 assistants, Business includes 10, and you can add more to any plan.

06How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days with no bank card: create your assistant, teach it your prices and let it answer real clients. When the 7 days end, choose a plan to keep it answering. Your account, your assistants and everything you wrote stay saved.
