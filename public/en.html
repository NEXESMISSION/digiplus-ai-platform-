01What is a "reply"?

Every message your assistant sends counts as one reply. Your clients' messages, and your own, are never counted. A normal sales conversation uses 5 to 10 replies.

02Can it invent prices?

No. It answers only with the services, prices and rules you wrote. When it does not know, it says so, shares your contact details and passes the question to you.

03Can I pay in Tunisian dinars?

Yes: D17 or bank transfer. Your plan is activated as soon as we confirm the payment. Clients abroad can pay by card in USD.

04What if I hit my monthly limit?

Your assistant stops answering and shows your contact details to clients, so nobody is left without an answer. Add a pack of 1,000 replies for 15 DT, or move to the plan above, and it starts again immediately.

05Can I run several businesses?

Yes. Each assistant has its own knowledge, link and conversations. Pro includes 3, Business 10, and you can add more to any plan.

06How do the 7 days work?

You test everything, with no bank card. At the end of the 7 days you choose a plan — otherwise the account simply pauses. Your assistant and everything you wrote stay saved.
